
## 1. A/B Testing 
Think of a traditional hypothesis testing problem. <br>
Suppose you want to know whether the average weight of cattle differs from 500kg. <br>
Then, with null: mu = 500, alternative: mu not= 500, you might use a one-sample t-test to test this hypothesis from your sample of cattle weights. <br>
